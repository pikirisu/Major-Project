# Pre-registration: leave-one-subject-out (LOSO) rows for the scoreboard (written 2026-11-17 14:55, before any LOSO run)

Written before any model below was fitted under LOSO. Every result is reported
whatever it turns out to be.

## Why

Three of the scoreboard's comparators were evaluated with leave-one-subject-out
cross-validation, while every number of ours uses 5-fold cross-validation by
patient (5 × 5 for AD vs HC and AD vs FTD):

| task | comparator | protocol | value |
|---|---|---|---|
| AD vs HC | Wang et al. 2026 [14] | LOSO | AUC 0.912, accuracy 87.7 % |
| AD vs HC | public benchmark [3] | LOSO | AUC 0.883, accuracy 84.6 % |
| AD vs FTD | Mlinarič et al. 2026 [4] | nested LOSO | AUC 0.651 |

These runs make the split match. They do not match the rest of each pipeline
(preprocessing, epoch length, features).

**Status of the results.** The 5 × 5 numbers stay the primary results in the
README, the deck and the report. The LOSO numbers are added as a second row,
labelled "LOSO (protocol-matched)". Neither replaces the other, and the better
of the two is never chosen.

## Common rules

- **LOSO.** Each patient of the task is the test set once. The model is fitted
  on the other patients of the task, so every patient gets one prediction from
  a model that never saw them.
- **Metrics.** Patient-level AUC (Mann–Whitney), plus accuracy, recall and
  specificity at a 0.5 cut, pooled over all test patients. Nothing is tuned on
  test patients, including the threshold.
- **CIs.** 95 % CI from 2000 bootstrap resamples of patients, each class
  resampled separately, seed 42. This is the method of Cell 11 and Experiment 10.
- **Verdict.** The scoreboard's rule. "Level" means the published value lies
  inside our 95 % CI. "Ahead" means it lies below the lower bound, and "behind"
  means it lies above the upper bound.

## Part 1: AD vs HC, band-power logistic regression (`LOSO_ds004504.ipynb`)

- **Data.** `subject_cache/`: 65 patients (36 AD, 29 HC), 95 band-power numbers
  per 20 s window. A patient's score is the mean of their window probabilities,
  as in Cell 5 of `GAT_ds004504.ipynb`.
- **Primary model.** StandardScaler, then L2 logistic regression with C = 0.1,
  `class_weight="balanced"` and max_iter 5000, fitted on all windows of the
  other 64 patients.
- **Why balanced weights.** Under LOSO, the class balance of the training set
  moves against the held-out patient's label in every fold. Leaving out an AD
  patient gives 35 AD vs 29 HC; leaving out an HC patient gives 36 vs 28. An
  unweighted model's intercept follows that shift. This biases the pooled AUC
  downward, and strong regularisation (C = 0.1) makes the intercept count for
  more. Balanced weights hold the effective prior fixed.
- **Identity check.** Cell 5's unweighted model on the 5 × 5 folds must
  reproduce the "LR floor" AUC and accuracy in `results_gat/statistics.json`
  exactly.
- **Secondary results.** These get no verdict; they explain the primary.
  - (a) Cell 5's unweighted model under LOSO. Primary minus (a) measures the
    prior-shift effect.
  - (b) The balanced model on the 5 × 5 folds. Primary minus (b) measures the
    effect of the split.
  - Both differences are paired patient bootstraps, reported with 95 % CIs and
    not Holm-corrected (descriptive).
- **Verdicts.** Reported against both AD-vs-HC comparators, for context. The
  scoreboard row is the GAT (Part 3), as it is for 5 × 5.

## Part 2: AD vs FTD, C3 lagged cross-correlation (`LOSO_ds004504.ipynb`)

- **Data.** Experiment 10's saved matrices,
  `experiments_ad_vs_ftd/features/c3_ds006036_matrices.npz`, unchanged:
  ds006036, 44 s per patient, 59 patients (36 AD, 23 FTD).
- **Pipeline.** Experiment 10's `run_fold`, unchanged.
  - The outer loop leaves one of the 59 patients out.
  - C is chosen from [0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1] by 5-fold inner stratified CV AUC
    on the other 58. This is nested LOSO, as in Mlinarič et al.
  - Inner seed = 42 + fold number (1–59).
- **Identity check.** The notebook's `run_fold` on Experiment 10's folds must
  reproduce Experiment 10's ds006036 scores exactly
  (`results/c3_ds006036/c3_oof.npz`).
- **Primary result.** The AUC of q_AD over the 59 patients, with its CI, and a
  verdict against Mlinarič 0.651. The scoreboard's AD-vs-FTD row is C3 on
  ds006036, so this is its LOSO counterpart.
- **Secondary results.**
  - (a) **Chance under LOSO.** 1000 shuffles of the 59 diagnoses through the
    whole LOSO pipeline. Reported: the one-sided permutation p, and the median
    and 95 % range of the shuffled AUCs. LOSO can push chance below 0.5, so its
    own chance level is part of the result.
  - (b) **C3 on ds004504, 300 s** (Experiment 9's matrices), under the same
    LOSO. This is the recording Mlinarič et al. used. C3 was picked on this
    recording after its results were seen, so (b) is labelled post hoc and
    gets no verdict.

## Part 3: the GAT, AD vs HC (`GAT_loso_ds004504.ipynb`; run only after approval, about 1.5–2 h on CPU)

- **What changes.** The notebook is `GAT_ds004504.ipynb` with only two cells
  changed:
  - **Cell 4, splits.** The outer loop leaves one of the 65 patients out.
    Validation patients come from the other 64 by the 5 × 5 rule: the first
    fold of StratifiedKFold(5, shuffle=True, random_state=42 + 1000 + fold
    number) over those patients (about 13). About 51 patients train, against
    about 42 in 5 × 5.
  - **Cell 5, the band-power model.** Part 1's balanced model. It must
    reproduce Part 1's predictions exactly.
- **What stays the same:**
  - the model;
  - learning rate 3e-3, cosine schedule over 80 epochs, warm-up 15, patience 20;
  - the epoch chosen on validation-patient AUC;
  - batch size 64, weight decay 1e-4, seed 42.
- **Soft vote.** The mean of the GAT's and the balanced model's
  probabilities, with equal weights (Cell 10's rule).
- **Seeds.** One training seed (42), and that run is the reported number.
  Seeds run later are reported next to it as training noise and never replace
  it. This is the same rule as the GAT re-run of 2026-11-15.
- **Statistics.** Only within LOSO: GAT vs band power, soft vote vs band power
  and soft vote vs GAT. Each is a paired patient bootstrap, with Holm
  correction over the three. There is no paired test against any 5 × 5 run
  (a different protocol); those are shown side by side only.
- **Primary result.** The GAT's LOSO AUC and accuracy with CIs, and verdicts
  against Wang 2026 (0.912 / 87.7 %) and the benchmark (0.883 / 84.6 %).

## Expectations (written before any run)

LOSO trains on more patients: about 64 instead of 52 for the logistic
regression, and about 51 instead of 42 for the GAT. A small rise, about
+0.01–0.02 AUC, is expected, and the scoreboard verdicts should not change.
A lower LOSO number is reported the same way as a higher one.

## Limits

- **One cohort.** All of this uses the same patients.
- **One partition.** LOSO has a single way to split the patients, so its
  uncertainty comes from patient sampling (the CI) and, for the GAT, from
  training noise (about ±0.015 AUC from one retraining).
- **Pipelines.** The comparators differ from us in preprocessing and features.
  The split now matches, but the rest of the pipeline does not.
