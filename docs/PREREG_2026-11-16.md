# Pre-registration: coupling graphs, a dementia model and the final scoreboard (written 2026-11-16, before any run)

Written before any coupling graph was computed and before any of the models
below was trained. Every result is reported whatever it turns out to be.
Experiments 9–11 (`experiments_ad_vs_ftd/`) come first and have their own
protocols. The two-recording C3 test (Experiment 11) is running while this is written.

## B: four coupling-graph twins of the 3-class GAT

The current GAT's two graphs, E1 and E2, are |Pearson| and MI computed between
electrodes' **power-spectrum vectors** in each 20 s window. They measure how
alike two electrodes' spectra are, not how the signals couple in time. These
twins replace both graphs with time-domain coupling, the kind of graph
DT-GCN (Liu & Meng 2026) used.

**What changes.** Each twin is `GAT_multiclass_ds004504.ipynb` with only
Cell 3 changed: E1 and E2 become one subject-level coupling graph per band,
used in both GAT layers.

**What stays identical:**
- the node features;
- the folds (StratifiedGroupKFold, seed 42 + 1000 r, 3 repeats × 5) and the seeds;
- the CNN, GAT and MLP;
- the learning rate 3e-3, 30 fixed epochs and the class-weighted loss;
- the band-power floor, the soft vote and the statistics cell.

| notebook | graph per band | what it measures | results |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GAT_multiclass_pearson_ds004504.ipynb` | \|Pearson r\| at zero lag | linear amplitude coupling (the measure behind C3) | `results_gat_multiclass_pearson/` |
| `GAT_multiclass_plv_ds004504.ipynb` | phase-locking value of the Hilbert phases | phase synchrony | `results_gat_multiclass_plv/` |
| `GAT_multiclass_aec_ds004504.ipynb` | orthogonalised amplitude-envelope correlation (AEC-c), symmetrised, \|r\| | amplitude coupling with zero-lag leakage removed | `results_gat_multiclass_aec/` |
| `GAT_multiclass_granger_ds004504.ipynb` | pairwise Granger causality F(j→i) = ln(σ²(x_i \| own past) / σ²(x_i \| own past + past of x_j)) | directed influence | `results_gat_multiclass_granger/` |

**Graphs** (`build_fc_graphs.ipynb` → `subject_cache_fc/fc_graphs.npz`):
- **Data.** C3's data: each subject's first 75 join-free 4 s epochs (300 s), with
  a common average reference. The cache's five bands (delta 0.5–4, theta 4–8,
  alpha 8–13, beta 13–30, gamma 30–45 Hz) use a zero-phase FIR on the continuous
  signal, then 250 Hz.
- **Averaging.** Each measure is computed per epoch and averaged over the 75 epochs.
- **Granger.** Computed once, broadband, on 125 Hz data with VAR order 10
  (80 ms) pooled over the 75 epochs. The same graph fills all five bands.
  - Band-limited VAR models are ill-conditioned.
  - Pairwise, because with an average reference each channel is a linear
    combination of the others, which makes conditional Granger zero.
- **Self-loop.** Each node's self-loop is its strongest connection (the row
  maximum of the off-diagonal). Values are floored at 1e-6.
- **Scale.** Row scale does not matter, because the attention adds log E_ij
  inside a softmax over j.
- **Copula.** DT-GCN's fourth graph is not used. Its released script fails
  before computing it (`EEG_data[:, i, 1000] = ...` on an axis of size 1000).
  A Gaussian copula would rank EEG edges almost exactly as |Pearson| does.

**Comparisons** (in `Final_scoreboard_ds004504.ipynb`, from saved
out-of-fold probabilities, paired over the same 88 subjects and folds):
- **Primary.** Each twin's subject macro recall minus the current GAT's
  (`results_gat_multiclass/`). Paired subject bootstrap of the repeat mean,
  Holm over the four twins.
- **Key secondary.** AD-vs-FTD AUC inside the 3-class model, the bottleneck.
  The same test, Holm over the four.
- **Reported for every twin:** accuracy, macro-F1, recall per class, macro AUC
  and dementia AUC, alone and soft-voted with band power.
- **Expectation.** Small or no gains.
  - The current GAT's AD-vs-FTD AUC is 0.595, and C3 (a linear model on a
    Pearson-type graph) reaches 0.63–0.64.
  - Re-running the binary GAT once moved its AUC by 0.014, so a difference
    of that size is noise.
- **Limitation, stated now.** The twins change both the graph type and its time
  scale (subject-level instead of per window), and this design cannot separate the two.

## C: a dedicated dementia-vs-HC model

`GAT_dementia_ds004504.ipynb`:
- **Model.** The 3-class GAT with AD and FTD pooled into one "dementia" label:
  88 subjects, 2 outputs, class-weighted loss.
- **Folds.** The same folds, built from the 3-class labels so that they match
  exactly. Everything else as in B.
- **Floor and soft vote.** The band-power floor and the soft vote use the same pooling.
- **Reported.** Subject accuracy (argmax), AUC, sensitivity and specificity,
  for the GAT alone and the soft vote. Also the 3-class model's by-product
  dementia score on the same folds.
- **Benchmark.** Stefanou et al. 2025, 80.7 % accuracy (quoted from the AHEPA
  review). Expectation: AUC about 0.87–0.89, as the 3-class by-product and its
  soft vote already reach.

## Final model and scoreboard (fixed now)

**The final 3-class model is the two-stage model**, on repeats 1–3 (where every
part shares folds):
- stage 1: P(dementia) from C's soft vote;
- stage 2: C3's q_AD, from ds006036 (Experiment 10), or the two-recording
  average if Experiment 11's pre-registered rule adopts it;
- P(HC) = 1 − p_d, P(AD) = p_d · q, P(FTD) = p_d · (1 − q).

It is compared with today's soft vote (paired subject bootstrap: macro recall,
accuracy, macro-F1). It uses both recordings of each patient (ds006036 for
stage 2), and the scoreboard says so. The B twins are reported beside it.
They do not change it.

**Scoreboard.** Each task against the best honestly evaluated published result
on ds004504. The bars and the 3-class averages are quoted from the AHEPA
review (Miltiadous et al. 2026):

| task | our number | published bar |
|---|---|---|
| AD vs HC | `results_gat/` (unchanged) | Wang et al. 2026: 87.7 %, AUC 0.912 |
| dementia vs HC | C | Stefanou et al. 2025: 80.7 % |
| AD vs FTD | Experiment 10 (and 11) | Mlinarič et al. 2026: AUC 0.651 |
| 3-class | the final model | AHEPA review average ≈ 70 % accuracy, F1 59.5 %; best quoted 76.0 % (not checked individually); strict GitHub benchmark macro recall 0.611 |

**Verdicts.** "Level" means the published value lies inside our 95 % CI.
"Ahead" or "behind" means it lies outside.

## Addendum, written 2026-11-16 at 21:10, before computing either: two combinations, no training

Both use saved out-of-fold outputs on repeats 1–3, where every input shares
folds. The notebook is `Combine_ds004504.ipynb`, writing to `results_final/combine/`.
Every component's own result was already known, but no combination of them was.

**1. A combined AD-vs-FTD score (candidate stage 2).** Three scores, each
pointing to "AD" when higher. Their pairwise Spearman r is 0.13–0.36.
- **The three scores:**
  - the 3-class GAT's P(AD) / (P(AD) + P(FTD));
  - C3 on ds006036, Experiment 10 (the adopted stage 2);
  - the aperiodic exponent of Experiment 8 (2 s windows, a raw feature with
    nothing fitted; its direction was fixed in that pre-registration).
- **The rule.** In each outer fold:
  - each score (probabilities as logits) is standardised with the mean and SD
    of the fold's **training** dementia patients;
  - the three z-scores are averaged with equal weights;
  - q_AD = Φ(average / its training SD).
  No weights are fitted and no other combination is tried. C3 on ds004504 is
  left out because it was picked after the fact.
- **Test.** AD-vs-FTD AUC (59 patients, mean of repeats 1–3) minus C3 alone,
  by paired bootstrap.
- **Adoption.** The combined score replaces C3 as stage 2, and the scoreboard's
  3-class rows are updated, only if the lower 95 % bound of that gain is above 0.

**2a. An ensemble of the five 3-class GATs** (PSD, Pearson, PLV, AEC-c and
Granger graphs): their window-level probabilities are averaged.
- **Comparisons.** The ensemble alone vs today's GAT, and the ensemble
  soft-voted with band power vs today's soft vote. Subject macro recall
  (primary) and AD-vs-FTD AUC, by paired bootstrap, Holm over the two comparisons.
- **Reporting.** It is reported beside the final model and does not replace it.
