# Pre-registration: closing review remarks 3 and 4 (written 2026-11-14, before any run)

Written before any GATv2 model was trained and before ds006036 was opened.
Every result below is reported whatever it turns out to be.

## Remark 3: GATv2 and dynamic (adaptive) graphs

Two twins of `GAT_ds004504.ipynb`. Cache, folds, CNN, MLP, training loop and
statistics are identical code; only the graph layer changes.

| notebook | graph layer | result folder |
|---|---|---|
| `GATv2_ds004504.ipynb` | GATv2 score `a·LeakyReLU(W_s x_i + W_d x_j) + log E_ij` | `results_gatv2/` |
| `GATv2gate_ds004504.ipynb` | the same, with the prior `E_ij` multiplied by a learned edge gate `σ(MLP[x_i, x_j, E_ij])` | `results_gatv2gate/` |

- **Learning rate:** for each new model, chosen on *validation* subjects only:
  repeat 1, five folds, candidates 1e-3 and 3e-3, criterion = mean of the best
  validation subject AUC per fold. If the two are within 0.005, keep 3e-3
  (the GAT's rate) for a like-for-like comparison. Test subjects are never used.
- **Main runs:** 5-fold CV grouped by subject × 5 repeats, the same seeds and
  folds as every other Case B model.
- **Comparisons:** GATv2 vs GAT, and gate vs GATv2, as paired subject-bootstrap
  differences in AUC, Holm-corrected within each notebook's family (Cell 11).
- **Expectation:** no significant difference. With 65 subjects an AUC has a
  95 % CI of about ±0.06, and the earlier 1-repeat ablation found GATv1 ≥ GATv2.
  A lead with a p (Holm) at or above 0.05 is reported as "no significant difference".

## Remark 4: a second dataset, ds006036

ds006036 (OpenNeuro, CC0): the same 88 people as ds004504, recorded with eyes
open during photic (flashing-light) stimulation, 19 channels. It is a second
recording condition, **not an independent cohort**, and is reported that way.

- **Preprocessing:** the identical `build_cache.ipynb` feature code (20 s windows,
  5 s step, Welch PSD at 0.2 Hz, Pearson and MI graphs, the same window caps),
  pointed at the ds006036 recordings.
- **Test 1, replication:** the GAT notebook unchanged, run on the ds006036 cache
  (AD vs HC, 65 subjects, 5-fold × 5 repeats, band-power logistic floor on the
  same folds).
- **Expectation:** lower than on eyes-closed rest (photic driving adds
  stimulus-locked peaks, and eyes-open alpha is suppressed). Subject AUC above
  0.5 with its CI excluding 0.5 counts as "the detection replicates".
